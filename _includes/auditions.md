Cast members are selected from the legal community or anyone whose work is substantially connected to the law, such as attorneys, judges, law clerks, paralegals, legal assistants, court personnel, law students, and other professionals and staff employed in the legal profession. Prior theatrical experience is welcome, but not required. No prior stage experience is necessary—enthusiasm, reliability, and a willingness to have fun are far more important!
